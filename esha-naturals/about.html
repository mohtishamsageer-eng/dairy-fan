Who we are

Natural goodness for your hair & home

Esha Naturals was created with one simple belief: everyday care should be pure, honest and close to nature. From the weekly champi our mothers and grandmothers swore by to the aroma of fresh tadka in the kitchen, natural oils have always been at the heart of Pakistani homes.

We bring you that same goodness, carefully prepared and bottled with care. Our hair oils combine trusted ingredients like amla, castor, coconut and ritha to nourish the scalp and strengthen hair. Our mustard and sesame oils bring rich aroma and authentic taste to your family's meals.

Every bottle carries our promise: 100% original, pure, natural and trusted.
