Shipping & Delivery

We deliver to cities and towns all over Pakistan.

  • Order confirmation: after you place your order, our team will call or WhatsApp you to confirm your details before dispatch.
  • Delivery time: your order is delivered within 3–4 working days after order confirmation. Remote areas may occasionally take a little longer.
  • Delivery charges: Rs 200 per order, and free delivery on orders of Rs 2,000 or more.
  • Delivery charges: a flat Rs 200 per order.
  • Delivery charges: free delivery on all orders.
  • Please check your details: make sure your mobile number and address are correct. If we cannot reach you to confirm, your order may be delayed.

Payment

We offer Cash on Delivery (COD) all over Pakistan. There is no advance payment. Simply pay the rider in cash when your order arrives. The total you pay is shown in your cart and on your order confirmation.

Returns & Exchanges

Your satisfaction matters to us. If your order arrives damaged, leaking or incorrect, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and a photo of the product, and we will arrange a replacement at no extra cost.

  • For hygiene and safety reasons, opened or used products cannot be returned unless they are faulty.
  • Please check your parcel at the time of delivery whenever possible.
  • Replacements are sent after our team has reviewed your request.

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. When you place an order we collect your name, mobile number, delivery address and, if you choose, your email address.

  • We use these details only to confirm, deliver and support your order.
  • We share your delivery details only with our delivery partners, and only so they can deliver your order. We never sell your information.
  • Your cart and your last delivery details are saved in your own browser so you don't have to type them again. You can remove them at any time by clearing your browser data.
  • To update or delete your information, please contact us.

Terms of Sale

  • All prices are in Pakistani Rupees (Rs). The price shown when you place your order is the price you pay.
  • Product images are for illustration. Packaging may vary slightly from the pictures.
  • Hair oils are for external use only. Please do a patch test before first use and avoid contact with eyes.
  • We may contact you to confirm your order and may cancel orders that cannot be confirmed.

Questions about these policies? Contact us and we will be happy to help.
